Welcome to CyberSafe Students

Learn simple and practical ways to protect yourself from common cybersecurity threats.

Why Cybersecurity Matters?

Students use smartphones, laptops, social media, online learning platforms and Internet services every day. Cybersecurity awareness helps protect personal information, accounts and digital identity.

🔐 Strong Passwords

Use long and unique passwords for your important accounts.

🎣 Avoid Phishing

Never click suspicious links or provide sensitive information to unknown people.

📱 Protect Social Media

Use privacy settings and avoid sharing unnecessary personal information online.

🌐 Browse Safely

Use trusted websites and keep your devices and applications updated.

🔐 Password Security

Use Strong Passwords

Create passwords that are long, unique and difficult to guess. Avoid names, birthdays and mobile numbers.

Use Different Passwords

Do not use the same password for every account. If one account is compromised, other accounts can also be at risk.

Enable Two-Factor Authentication

Two-Factor Authentication provides an additional layer of security to your online accounts.

Never Share Passwords

Never share your password, PIN or OTP with other people.

🎣 Phishing Awareness

Phishing is a cyberattack technique where attackers try to trick people into revealing sensitive information or clicking malicious links.

⚠️ Urgent Messages

Be careful with messages saying "Act Now", "Account Blocked" or "Claim Your Prize".

🔗 Suspicious Links

Do not click unknown or suspicious links received through messages or emails.

📧 Unknown Senders

Be cautious when receiving unexpected messages from unknown senders.

🔑 Personal Information

Never provide passwords, OTPs or sensitive information in response to suspicious requests.

Remember:

STOP → CHECK → THINK → THEN CLICK

📱 Social Media Safety

Protect Your Privacy

Review privacy settings and control who can see your posts and personal information.

Avoid Oversharing

Do not unnecessarily share your address, phone number, location or other personal information.

Beware of Fake Profiles

Be careful when accepting friend or follow requests from unknown accounts.

Secure Your Account

Use a strong password and enable Two-Factor Authentication.

🌐 Safe Internet Usage

🔄 Update Software

Keep your operating system, browser and applications updated.

📥 Safe Downloads

Download applications and files only from trusted sources.

📶 Public Wi-Fi

Be careful when using unknown public Wi-Fi networks, especially for sensitive activities.

🌍 Check Websites

Check the website address carefully before entering personal or sensitive information.
